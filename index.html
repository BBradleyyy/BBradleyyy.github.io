About

Who I am

I'm Bradley (Dogukan) Kakiz, a Financial Mathematics & Economics and Statistics student at the University of Victoria, graduating in 2028, with a focus on credit, development finance and valuation.

As a financial analyst in development finance and credit, I build pro formas and equity waterfalls for real estate developments, and stress-test them to find the debt a project can actually support. At UVic's Impact Investing Hub I worked on DSCR and leverage analysis for clean-energy and First Nations utility financing. As fund manager at Propel Impact, UVic's student-run impact fund, I designed our screening framework and lead a team of associates. In January 2027 I join UVic's Treasury Department as a Treasury & Investment Associate.

Outside work I build SignalNorth, an open-source platform that turns SEC filings and deal terms into formula-driven Excel models, each checked line by line against an independent engine. I'm preparing for CFA Level I.

Away from finance: basketball (national runner-up, three provincial titles), guitar and piano, and chess.
